- **lens:** a shared principle emerging across rooms · **strength: 2 streams**
- `home/bathroom` — chose **terracotta** (warm), and was pleased it felt deliberate, not random.
- `home/kitchen` — chose **deep green** *specifically to carry the bathroom's warm tone through* rather than start each room fresh (LOG 2026-06-02).
- **emergent hypothesis [inference]:** there's an unspoken house-wide rule forming — *every room should pull from one warm palette so the home reads as a whole.* Neither room's notes state it as a rule; it only appears when you hold the two side by side. Worth making explicit before the next room is chosen — it'd decide the splashback (kitchen Q2) and any future room up front.
- **status:** `ripening` — surfaced for the owner to confirm: is "one warm palette, house-wide" a real principle you want to adopt? If yes, it becomes a decision the next rooms inherit.

*This is what orchestrate is for: not finding similar words, but noticing a principle you're following without having named it — so you can decide it on purpose.*
